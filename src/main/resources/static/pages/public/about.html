About SmartCare

SmartCare is a portfolio/academic project built to demonstrate a realistic hospital appointment and queue management platform — not a production medical system, and not affiliated with any real hospital.

The department and slot suggestions in this app are rule-based appointment guidance only. They do not diagnose any medical condition. Always consult a qualified doctor for medical advice.

What it does

Patients can search for doctors, get a department suggestion from a short symptom list, book a slot, pay online (via a mock gateway for this demo), check in with a QR code, and track their live position in the queue. Doctors manage their schedule, run consultations, and issue digital prescriptions. Admins oversee doctors, departments, and hospital-wide analytics.

What it isn't

This project does not integrate with ABHA/ABDM or any real national health record system, and its payment flow is a mock gateway with no real transactions or stored card data.
